The workbench

I learn by building systems that solve real problems.

Some started as coursework. Some started because something around the house annoyed me. The interesting part is what happens after the first version works.

Featured build // active

CollectorVision

A custom collection-management and image-recognition workflow built around a real Hot Wheels collection. It combines catalog data, multiple vehicle images, OCR, image processing, review workflows, image fingerprints, and a SQLite-backed application.

PythonSQLiteOCRImage processingLinuxSelf-hosted

Why it matters: it turns imperfect photos and incomplete data into a maintainable workflow rather than a one-off OCR demo.
