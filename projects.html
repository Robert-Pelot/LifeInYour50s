From Disk Image to Defensible Findings

A sanitized forensic case study built from a 39-page evidence packet and an E01 image. The focus is not the underlying case narrative, but the examination process: verify integrity, document artifacts, correlate evidence, respect metadata limits, and report only what the data can support.

FTK ImagerE01HashingMetadataEvidence correlationReporting
