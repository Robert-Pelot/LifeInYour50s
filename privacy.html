Plain-English policy

Privacy.

This is a personal showcase site, not an app that needs an account, profile, or pile of tracking data.

What this site collects

The site itself does not include user accounts, a comment system, a contact form, or advertising trackers. If you choose to contact me by email, your message is handled by your email provider and mine.

Hosting

The site is hosted using third-party infrastructure. Like most web hosts, the hosting platform may maintain technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Future changes

If I later add analytics, a newsletter, a contact form, or another feature that changes what data is collected, I will update this page to describe it.
