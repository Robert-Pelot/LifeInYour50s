The physical and virtual environment behind the projects.

Not one server. A working technical ecosystem.

Proxmox is the core server platform, but the lab is bigger than Proxmox: a multi-monitor primary workstation, laptop, mini test PC, Raspberry Pi, personal network edge, storage, monitoring, IoT experiments, and purpose-built virtual labs all work together.

Physical systems

Different machines. Different jobs.

The point is not to make every device powerful. It is to have the right place to develop, test, break, isolate, monitor, and learn.

Beyond infrastructure

The lab exists to build things.

Infrastructure becomes much more interesting when it is supporting software, local AI, school labs, monitoring, and experiments that evolve over time.

IoT & edge

The network does not stop at computers.

The environment also includes cameras, a thermostat, smart switches, and small MQTT experiments such as temperature, humidity, and weather-related sensor projects. The Raspberry Pi and breadboard work give me a place to test the edge between software, networks, and physical devices.

Next expansion

Cisco lab gear is waiting in the wings.

Four Cisco switches are on hand for future integration, and I am looking for suitable Cisco routers to build a more deliberate routing and switching lab that ties into the existing environment rather than living as an isolated classroom exercise.
